HOW IT WORKS

Downed, not dead. Lethal damage downs you unless it is a headshot or catastrophic. Downed players crawl, cannot shoot and bleed out in 20s. Teammates drag you to cover and revive you with a medical kit (6s). Each player can be rescued once per round — after that, there is no second chance.

Injuries. Arm hits add recoil and sway. Leg hits slow you and disable sprinting. Chest and limb hits can cause bleeding — bandage light bleeding, tourniquet heavy limb bleeding.

Gunplay. First shots are accurate when you stand still. Moving ruins accuracy; crouching helps. Every rifle has a fixed spray pattern: pull down and against the drift.
