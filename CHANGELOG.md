# Changelog

## v1.0.0
- **Realistic visuals.** The block placeholders are replaced with real 3D art: rigged and animated soldiers in camo uniforms, helmets and plate carriers. They idle, walk, run, crouch, shoot, revive on one knee, drag teammates, go down and die. The first-person AK, M4 and pistol have gloved arms that hold the gun with IK. The warehouse is dressed with shipping containers, crate stacks, pallets, steel shelving, barriers, barrels, a generator, a pallet truck and more.
- Rendering: PBR materials with normal and roughness maps, an HDRI environment, ACES tone mapping, soft sun shadows through the roof skylights, light shafts in the haze, hanging lamps, GTAO ambient occlusion, bloom, SMAA/FXAA and muzzle-flash lights. Graphics presets are Low, Medium and High.
- First playable prototype of NO SECOND CHANCE: **The Warehouse**, 5v5 against bots (attackers vs defenders, A and B bomb sites).
- CS-style hitscan gunplay: AK-74, M4, P9 and R50 pistols with fixed spray patterns, first-shot accuracy, movement and jump inaccuracy, crouch bonus, recoil kick, tracers, decals and hit markers.
- Injuries: head / chest / stomach / arm / leg hit zones, armor and helmet, arm hits add recoil and sway, leg hits slow you and stop sprinting, light and heavy bleeding (bandage / tourniquet), Healthy / Wounded / Critical states.
- **Downed, not dead:** 20 s bleedout, crawl, teammates drag you (hold G) and revive you with a medkit (hold E, 6 s). One rescue per player per round; enemies can finish downed players.
- Round system: buy phase, prep, 2:00 rounds, 4 s plant, 40 s bomb, 7 s defuse, first to 7, sides swap after 6 rounds. Economy with win/loss streak bonus, kill, plant, defuse and rescue rewards, magazine memory, frag / smoke / flash.
- Bots that navigate, take cover, use utility, plant, hold, rotate, retake, defuse and rescue teammates (easy / normal / hard).
- Tactical HUD: score, timer, bomb status, body-injury display, weapon / magazines, team status, kill feed, round summary, Tab scoreboard, radar, buy menu.
- Windows launcher with automatic game updates from GitHub Releases and file-based saves in %APPDATA%\NoSecondChance.
