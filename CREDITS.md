# Credits

NO SECOND CHANCE uses only free assets that may be redistributed. Every third-party 3D model, texture and HDRI below is **CC0 (public domain)**, so none of them legally needs attribution. They are credited here anyway, with thanks to their authors. The models were converted and compressed for the web (glTF + meshopt + WebP), and the soldier rig was re-skinned with a procedural camouflage uniform, but the source art is theirs.

## Code and fonts
- [three.js](https://threejs.org) r187: MIT licence, vendored in `js/vendor/`. Includes the GLTFLoader, HDRLoader, meshopt decoder, EffectComposer, GTAO, UnrealBloom, SMAA/FXAA and output passes from `examples/jsm`.
- Fonts: [Barlow and Barlow Condensed](https://github.com/jpt/barlow) by Jeremy Tribby, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

## Characters and animation (CC0)
- Soldier body and skeleton: [Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com) by **Quaternius**.
- Animations (idle, walk, jog, sprint, crouch, kneeling revive, carry/drag, death, lying): [Universal Animation Library](https://quaternius.com) and **Universal Animation Library 2** by **Quaternius**.
- The helmet, plate carrier, pouches and armbands are procedural, made for this game.
